# Reaper Art Manifest

Original dark-fantasy artwork generated for Gilded Fate's Reaper expansion.

## Standalone assets

- `ReaperCharacter.png` — transparent full-body Reaper character portrait.
- `DeathsKeepsake.png` — transparent floating icon for the Reaper-exclusive starting relic.

## Card atlases

Card scenes have no baked-in text or frames. Runtime card frames, costs, names, type banners, and rules text remain readable and reusable in the UI.
