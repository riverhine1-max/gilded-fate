VIDEO / STREAMING NOTICE
Scott Buckley requests attribution in YouTube video descriptions. Missing credits
may result in a Content ID claim. For trailers/playthroughs, copy the music credit
above and include the titles used. The Audio settings music slider can mute music
independently. Do not submit these recordings to Content ID or redistribute them
as a standalone soundtrack. Preserve these credits when distributing the game.
