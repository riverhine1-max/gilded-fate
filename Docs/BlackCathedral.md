# Black Cathedral (Act 3, Theme 1) - identity: JUDGMENT

Act 3 now rolls the Vault or the Black Cathedral. Every Judgment is deterministic, reads only the PREVIOUS player turn (cards played by type, unused Energy, Block at end of turn), and only chooses the enemy's next move. Each judging enemy shows a tooltip naming exactly what it watches. No interrupts; RNG only for Command and support ties.

Roster: Penitent Guard, Choir Warden (Support), Confessor, Censer Bearer, Reliquary Saint + Chapel Effigy, Execution Herald (visible Sentence 3/2/1), Oathbreaker Priest, Living Icon. Elites: High Confessor, Choir Eternal (Voices of Blade/Mercy/Vigil), Bell of Sentence (Toll 0/4). Boss: The Final Bishop (3 phases at <=260 and <=130 HP; the Voices are removed at the 1->2 transition with no heal/Strength/Block).

Act 3 rewards: normal 24 Gold, elite 48, boss 125; relic 5% normal / 60% elite; elite shard 11%.

## Battlefield rules (apply to every theme)
- Max 4 living creatures per combat. A summon that has no free spot is illegal and the owner uses its fallback move.
- Minions stand in FRONT of their owner/boss (nearest the hero); summoned Minions join in front of their owner.
- Enemy size follows importance: bosses largest, elites large, normals mid, Minions small. Lane width and hit region follow the drawn size.
