## Cross-character continuity

Vanguard's return must include the ability to hear his friend. Hexer's method must keep testimony that can contradict a plan. Reaper's care must leave the surviving existence an open future. These three requirements converge on Chapter X's unfinished search without staging its outcome or preempting the Narrator's self-recognition.

Hexer's uncertainty about global chronology does not deny Vanguard's local memory of turning away or the known cause of the Severing. The changed shield placement and the two notes of a recurring hour are bounded observations, not proof that a choice breaks a run loop. Reaper's refuge accounts add coexistence without granting the Plane a restoration power. His final conditional statement leaves the reversibility of fusion unknown.

The fourth companion remains a real friend who chose a dangerous protective erasure, stayed at the catastrophe, and survived fused with the Being. Early kindness does not excuse the later controlling decision. No new Being line, personality replacement, third consciousness, rescue method, villain origin, universal memory law, or new ending is introduced.
