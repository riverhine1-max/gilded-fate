# The Warning He Remembered Wrong

Hexer · Fate Debt 4 · MEM_14 · MAJOR

The scholar recovered a second sheet beneath the proposed memory erasure. It listed the observations needed to tell whether a connection had weakened. Several depended upon recollections the plan intended to remove. He had seen the contradiction, then remembered it afterward as a warning about incomplete execution.

~~The warning meant that sufficiently complete memory loss would end the danger.~~

**Complete memory loss could also remove the evidence needed to discover that the danger remained.**

A blank measurement would not establish a broken connection if the person making it could no longer recognize what had been measured. The scholar's question at the sanctuary was larger than he later recalled. It concerned both the failure of the plan and their ability to know whether it had failed.

The fourth companion's decision remains his own. Urgency did not become certainty merely because there was little time. The scholar had understood a possible route out of the danger; he had not established a safe way to verify its result. Losing the witness was itself one of the costs.

He had warned of more than a thread refusing to break. He had warned of no longer knowing where to look.
