# The Echo That Wasn't Empty

Reaper · Fate Debt 1 · MEM_03 · MAJOR

One echo on the abandoned road repeated a small movement: it shifted aside where no traveler passed. The Reaper first took it for the last trace of a journey, a meaningless habit remaining after the life had thinned beyond recognition. He watched the space the movement made.

~~An echo without a name had nothing left to recognize.~~

**It retained the habit of making room for someone beside it.**

The step was not simply forward. It left another person's share of the road clear. Whenever the impression faltered, that space appeared again. The lost name did not tell him who had been expected there. The movement told him that the life had once included someone other than itself.

He could not restore the absent traveler by inventing a name. He could record the relationship the echo still described. His earlier account had measured identity by the parts he could label, then called the rest empty. This fragment required a gentler discipline: attend to what it could express before deciding what it lacked.

He left the name unknown. He no longer left the life unacknowledged.
