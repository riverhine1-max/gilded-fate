# The Fourth Shadow

Reaper · Fate Debt 3 · MEM_09 · SMALL

Before the fourth shadow crossed the threshold, it waited. The Reaper had paused to examine a trace behind the others. The shadow held its place until he turned toward the passage, then moved with him. The recovered motion was so ordinary that it had almost escaped the account.

An imitation might have followed the three shapes ahead. This delay concerned the person who had fallen behind. The fourth presence had made room for someone whose attention was elsewhere.

What he recovered was more than a missing count. Someone had noticed him waiting.
