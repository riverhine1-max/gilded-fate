# The Prison Built from Mercy

Reaper · Fate Debt 4 · MEM_12 · MAJOR

Within one preserved future, a hand kept reaching toward a doorway. The frame remained clear. Nothing of the room beyond it could be recovered. The Reaper followed the associated traces and found that the reaching and the welcome it expected had been held apart. Each fragment continued without the other.

~~Everything held beneath the sanctuary had been saved from loss.~~

**Some fragments were kept after the relationships that gave them meaning had been torn apart.**

A doorway was not a home merely because its outline endured. The repeated gesture belonged to someone, and had once been answered by someone. The traveler's collection retained evidence of both while denying this impression the meeting that made them intelligible. Keeping more fragments had not made this particular life whole.

The Reaper understood the fear that had built the sanctuary. He also understood why counting preserved shapes could conceal another kind of loss. His record would keep the separation visible. If the fragments could ever be helped, that work would have to consider what had been taken between them, not merely what had been stored.

The prison had preserved the reach. It had not preserved the possibility of being met.
