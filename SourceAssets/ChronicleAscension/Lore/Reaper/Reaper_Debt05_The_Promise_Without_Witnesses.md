# The Promise Without Witnesses

Reaper · Fate Debt 5 · MEM_15 · SMALL

After promising to remember, the fourth companion followed the others' departing steps with his hand against the stone. Three different rhythms passed beneath his fingers. The Reaper remembered him repeating them once after the footsteps themselves had faded.

He had tried to keep more than a list of names. He was attending to the ways the others moved when no explanation was needed. The attempt makes the promise more human. It offers no proof that a human memory could withstand what followed.
