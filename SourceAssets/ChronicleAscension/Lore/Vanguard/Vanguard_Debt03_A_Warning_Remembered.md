# A Warning Remembered

Vanguard · Fate Debt 3 · MEM_04 · SMALL

At the gate, the warrior watched people begin answering the traveler's questions with the answers they hoped to hear. A warning that proved useful once became a reason to neglect the next uncertainty. He remembered a guard turning away an ordinary report because it had not appeared among the stranger's predictions.

The warrior kept that report. Nothing disastrous followed that day. That was why the habit was so easy to dismiss.

Trust had begun to decide which warnings were allowed to count.

## New information
