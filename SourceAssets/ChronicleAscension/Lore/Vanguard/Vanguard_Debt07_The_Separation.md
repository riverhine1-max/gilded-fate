# The Separation

Vanguard · Fate Debt 7 · MEM_16 · MAJOR

The warrior's last backward glance had become the whole of this memory. Beyond its edge, others were still trying to leave. One had lost a shoe. Another could not lift the arm that held a borrowed cloak. The warrior reached them while the voice behind the barrier was still calling.

~~He left because his friend's command mattered more than his own conviction.~~

**He left to lead the others out, without consenting to the separation he could not prevent.**

He did not stop arguing when he turned. He kept trying to look back while moving a frightened survivor ahead of him. The movement was awkward, divided between two duties. It was neither the obedience of a follower nor the clean decision his later guilt insisted it must have been.

This does not make the loss just. It does not prove that another choice would have saved everyone. It restores the people his account had cropped away. While one friend remained beyond his reach, others were within it. He could fail to keep them together and still have acted for more than himself.

The separation was real. So were the lives he could still reach when he turned away.

## New information

His departure included immediate responsibility for vulnerable survivors, and did not express agreement with erasure or abandonment.
