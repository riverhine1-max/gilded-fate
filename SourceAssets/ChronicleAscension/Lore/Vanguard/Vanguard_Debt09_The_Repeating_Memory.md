# The Repeating Memory

Vanguard · Fate Debt 9 · MEM_22 · SMALL

In one returning battle, the warrior remembered where the first blow would fall. He also remembered stepping toward it. This time he noticed someone behind his shoulder before he moved. He placed his shield differently.

The danger felt familiar; the person he protected did not. He could not tell whether this was another hour, another path, or a memory borrowing the shape of both. He recorded the difference instead of discarding it.

Recognition gave him a moment to consider. It did not tell him what he owed that moment.
