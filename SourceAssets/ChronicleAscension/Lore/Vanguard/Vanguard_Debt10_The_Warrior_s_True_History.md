# The Warrior's True History

Vanguard · Fate Debt 10 · MEM_25 · MAJOR

The repaired cup returns at last with the conversation that surrounded it. On an earlier road, after one of them had been forced to go ahead, the two men had agreed that distance would not excuse forgetting the other. They would turn back when turning back became possible. Neither had imagined the breaking.

~~The promise required the warrior to remain beside his friend, whatever happened.~~

**The promise bound him to return, not to make their deaths the same.**

At the barrier he had remembered only the shame of moving away. The agreement had also contained a task for the person who survived: carry the unfinished obligation beyond the place where help had become impossible. Going on could leave a debt unpaid without declaring it worthless.

Returning would demand more than recovering a face. The warrior would have to find his friend, hear what remained of him, and allow an answer he could not choose in advance. Their old disagreement was part of the friendship he sought to recover. A rescue that erased it would repeat the harm.

He had not lived merely to remember whom he lost. He had lived long enough to try to reach him again.

## New information

The earlier agreement was an obligation to return when possible, rather than a demand for shared death or compulsory restoration. Its fulfillment requires hearing the surviving friend's wishes.
